PRIVACY POLICY

Effective from 9 November 2026

This policy explains what happens to your data when you play Voidtide or visit lyravellgames.cz. The short version: the game does not collect your personal data, has no ads and no analytics, and does not track you.

Who we are

Lyravell Games is the studio brand of Vlastislav Platl, sole trader, Kamenný vrch 5272, 430 04 Chomutov, Czech Republic, Company ID (IČO) 76644049. He is the controller of any personal data described here.
Contact: info@lyravellgames.cz

What the game does not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting to us or to anyone else.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, unlocks and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. On Android, uninstalling the game removes them. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include them in the backup in your own Google account; we have no access to it. On Windows the save data stays in your user profile until you delete it.

If a game controller is connected, the game writes a small text file with the controller names to its local data folder. It is used only to troubleshoot controller problems, it never leaves your device, and we only see it if you choose to send it to us.

Purchases on Google Play

On Android you can try part of the game for free and unlock the full game with a one-time in-app purchase. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment card or bank details.

To complete a purchase, the game talks to Google Play on your device and stores locally that you own the full version. Google Play also provides us, as the seller, with the order details we need to deliver the purchase, handle refunds and meet our tax obligations (for example the order number, product, price, date and country). Google processes your data as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The Android app requests these permissions, used only for Google Play purchases:

PermissionWhy
Internet, network stateto connect to Google Play when you buy or restore the full version
Google Play billingto make the in-app purchase

The game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity's in-app purchasing library to talk to Google Play. We have not enabled any Unity services that send data to Unity (analytics, diagnostics or crash reporting are switched off).

If you email us

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to handle your request (legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you). We keep the correspondence only as long as needed, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

This website

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and served through Cloudflare. Like any web server, they process your IP address and technical request data to deliver the page and protect it against attacks, under their own privacy policies. We do not receive or use these logs.

Legal basis and retention

Order details from Google Play are processed to perform the purchase contract and to comply with accounting and tax law, and are kept for the period required by Czech law. Nothing else about you is stored by us.

Transfers outside the EU

Google, GitHub and Cloudflare may process data outside the European Union. They rely on safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

Children

The game is not specifically directed at children. It collects no personal data from any player, including children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing and to data portability. Write to info@lyravellgames.cz. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).

Changes

If the game or this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
