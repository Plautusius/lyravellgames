THE GAME

Every run starts quietly. A few enemies, one weapon, a long way to go. Then the tide rises — until they come from every side. Level up, pick your upgrades, fuse weapons, accept the blessings of the gods and face monstrous bosses on your way to the Void Lord.

  • 7heroes, each with a different style of play
  • 5worlds, from the Void Realm to the Inferno
  • 8weapons and 11 fusions that combine them
  • 5gods who offer their boons along the way
  • 36kinds of enemies, mini-bosses and bosses
